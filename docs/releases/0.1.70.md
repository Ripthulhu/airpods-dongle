# AirPods Dongle 0.1.70

Use one AAC-ELD access unit per packet for direct USB music in both Game Mode
states. Match LibrePods' packet sizing and remove the packet grouping change
during a toggle.

Keep the scheduled AAC-ELD deadlines through brief delays. A late submission
cannot make a slow clock adjustment report success before it catches up. A long
slow-clock gap cancels the adjustment, while the fast clock can continue from a
fresh deadline within the existing adjustment timeout. PCM32 and pitch-preserving
USB input remain in use. The phone relay and calls retain their existing packet
grouping, and volume, bitrate, pairing and controller settings are unaffected.

Regression tests cover packet sizing in both modes, deadline phase across timer
wrap, late sends, PCM continuity, pitch, and the unchanged relay packet path.
Live listening and weak-signal qualification are pending.
